Everything you need to practise properly

Simple to use on a phone or a laptop, and built around finding — and fixing — your weak spots.

⏱️

Real timed tests

A countdown timer, question palette, and Mark for Review, just like an exam hall. When the timer hits zero your answers are submitted for you.

🧩

Topic-wise tests

Every subject can have topic tests inside it — short focused tests on one topic, plus the Full Subject Test for the whole thing.

💾

Never lose progress

Your answers are saved as you go. If the page reloads or the browser closes, open the test again and carry on where you stopped.

📊

Result analysis

See your score and percentage, your practice rank, and how much time you spent on each question. Full tests also show a topic-wise breakdown, weakest first.

🔁

My Mistakes & revision

Wrong and unattempted questions are collected for revision, with the type of mistake. Revision opens one day after the original test.

🔔

Reminders & emails

Get your result by email with retake and revision links, set reminders (once, hourly, daily, weekly, monthly) or tap Remind me later on an unfinished test.

One subject, more than one exam

GATE and ECET share many subjects. So on the Home page a subject shows only its name and how many topic tests it has — the exam is shown inside.

  • Tap Choose Test on a subject to open its list.
  • Each exam has its own block — GATE, ECET or any other exam the subject is used for.
  • An exam with topic tests lists its Full Subject Test first, then the topic tests numbered 1, 2, 3 …
  • An exam with only one test simply shows it as the Full Subject Test.
  • Every test keeps its own best score and its own waiting time, so finishing one topic never locks the others.

How a test works

From opening a subject to reading your analysis, in five steps.

  1. Pick a subjectSearch for it or scroll the Home page, then tap Choose Test or Open Exam.
  2. Choose a testFull Subject Test for an exam, or one numbered topic test.
  3. Enter the passwordEach subject has a password. You enter it once per visit.
  4. Take the testAnswer, mark for review, jump between questions. The timer runs at 1 minute per question.
  5. Study the resultScore, rank, time per question, topic-wise view and your list of mistakes.

Good to know

A few rules that keep practice honest.

⏳
Waiting time after a testAfter you finish a test it locks for a while — 24 hours by default — before you can attempt it again. The admin can change this, so the lock message always shows the time left.
🔑
Subject passwordsPasswords are given by your admin or teacher. Once you enter it for a subject, its other tests open without asking again during that visit.
▶️
Unfinished testsA test in progress can always be resumed, even while other attempts are waiting. It shows as “In progress” with a Resume button.
🚫
No negative markingEvery question is worth the same and wrong answers cost nothing, so it is always worth answering.

Questions people ask

Quick answers.

Why is a test showing “Locked”?

You finished it recently. Tests wait for a set time before they can be attempted again — the time left is shown next to the lock. Other tests of the same subject are not affected.

What is the difference between a Full Subject Test and a topic test?

A topic test covers one topic only and is short. The Full Subject Test covers every question of that exam for the subject. Both have separate scores and separate waiting times.

I closed the browser in the middle of a test. Is it lost?

No. Answers are saved on your device as you go. Open the same test again and choose Resume.

Where do I see my earlier attempts?

In My Dashboard you will find your attempts and improvement, and in My Mistakes the questions you got wrong or skipped, ready for revision.

Why can’t I see a subject’s questions yet?

Some subjects are marked “Coming Soon” until their question bank is ready. New subjects and topic tests appear on Home as soon as they are added — tap Refresh Data if you don’t see them.

The password doesn’t work.

Passwords belong to the subject, not to you. Ask your admin or teacher for the current one.
