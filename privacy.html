1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to anyone who uses the Online Mock Test website, its installed app/PWA version, or takes a practice exam, uses reminders, or contacts the admin through it. It is operated by [YOUR BUSINESS / INDIVIDUAL NAME] ("we", "us").

2. What we collect

We collect the minimum needed to run the Service. Specifically:

3. What we do NOT collect

  • No phone number, home address, date of birth, or government ID
  • No payment or card details — the Service does not process payments (see the Refund Policy)
  • No device fingerprinting, no advertising identifiers, no location tracking
  • No analytics or tracking cookies, and no advertising/marketing cookies of any kind — see the Cookie Policy for exactly what local storage we do use
  • No social media login, so we never receive data from a Google/Facebook/etc. account beyond what you type into our own form

4. Why we collect it

  • To provide the Service itself — without your email, there is no way to show you "your" dashboard, mistakes, or history on a later visit, since the app has no separate login/password system.
  • To email you your result and, if you ask for one, a reminder.
  • To calculate a practice rank against other users of the same subject (only an anonymous count/position is shown to others — your name and email are never shown to other students).
  • To enforce the one-attempt-per-day cooldown on a subject, which needs to know when you last attempted it.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the exam/revision/reminder features described above.

6. Where your data is stored

Your account and exam data are stored in a Google Sheets spreadsheet accessed through a Google Apps Script backend, both operated on Google's cloud infrastructure. A separate copy of your own device's data (your saved answers mid-exam, cached dashboard numbers, and any exam result waiting to sync while offline) is stored locally on your device using localStorage — see the Cookie Policy for details on that.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account and exam history for as long as you continue using the Service, so your dashboard and progress remain available across visits. If you delete your account (Profile → Delete Account), your Name, Email, and every row of exam results, answers, mistakes, reminders, and history tied to that email are removed from our records — this is an actual, working feature already built into the app, not a manual request process.

8. Your rights

  • Access — your Profile page already shows everything we hold about your performance and account.
  • Correction — you can change your display name any time from Profile → Edit Profile.
  • Erasure — Profile → Delete Account permanently removes your data from our systems, immediately, without needing to contact anyone.
  • Withdraw consent — deleting your account also withdraws consent for further processing; we will not create a new record for that email unless you use the Service again and re-consent.
  • Questions or a request we haven't automated — contact us using the details in Section 13.

9. Children's data

The Service is aimed at students preparing for competitive/technical entrance exams and may be used by minors. We do not knowingly collect more data from a minor than from any other user (the same name + email, nothing extra), and we do not show ads or run any marketing to anyone. 10. Security

Data in transit between your device and our backend is encrypted (HTTPS). Access to the underlying spreadsheet/backend is restricted to the operator's own Google account. As with any online service, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security — but we do not store passwords (there is no password-based login for students) or payment details, which limits what could be exposed in the event of a breach.
