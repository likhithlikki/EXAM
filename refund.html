Refund Policy

Last updated: [DATE YOU PUBLISH THIS]

Current status: this app does not charge for anything. Every subject, mock test, revision test, dashboard, and reminder feature described on this site is free to use. There is no in-app purchase, subscription, or payment flow anywhere in the Service today, so there is nothing to request a refund for.

Why this page exists anyway

We're publishing this policy now, even though the Service is currently free, so that:

  • App marketplaces (like Google Play) that expect a refund policy link have one to check, accurately reflecting today's reality;
  • If paid features are ever introduced, there's a clear, pre-existing commitment for how refunds would work, rather than inventing terms after the fact.

If paid features are introduced in the future

Should the Service ever introduce a paid feature, subscription, or one-time purchase, this page will be updated before that feature launches, with clear terms covering at minimum:

  • What is/isn't refundable, and the time window for requesting one;
  • How to request a refund (contact method, required information);
  • How long refunds take to process once approved;
  • Any circumstances where a refund would not be granted (e.g., after a service has already been substantially used, where applicable law allows this).
