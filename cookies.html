Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Last updated: [DATE YOU PUBLISH THIS]

Short version: this app does not use cookies at all — no session cookies, no tracking cookies, no advertising cookies, no third-party cookies. It uses your browser's localStorage (a similar but different browser feature) to save your progress and preferences on your own device only. Nothing described here is shared between users or sent to an advertiser.

Why there's no cookie-consent banner

Cookie-consent banners exist because cookies (and similar tracking technologies) are often used to track people across sites for advertising, or to set session identifiers a server reads on every request. This app does neither: it sets no HTTP cookies whatsoever, and everything below is stored locally on your device and read only by this app, for its own functionality — not shared with us until you take an action that sends it (like submitting a test).

What's stored in your browser's localStorage

Key (prefix)What it holdsWhy
ecet_profileYour name and emailSo you don't have to retype them every visit
ecet_progress_*Your in-progress exam answers, timer stateSo closing the tab mid-exam doesn't lose your answers
ecet_dashboard_cache, ecet_mistakes_cache, ecet_reminders_cache, ecet_customsubjects_cache, ecet_profiledata_cacheA local copy of your own stats, mistakes, reminders, and subject listSo the app can show something instantly while it refreshes from the server, and still show your last-known data if you're offline
ecet_bankcache_*A copy of a subject's questionsSo that subject can be opened and taken with no internet connection, once you've opened it online at least once
ecet_outboxA finished exam result that hasn't reached our server yetSo an exam finished offline isn't lost — it uploads automatically once you're back online
ecet_admin_unlockedWhether this device has unlocked the Admin panelLocal convenience only — the real access check happens on the server

None of this data leaves your device except when the app itself sends it to our backend as part of using a feature (e.g. submitting a test sends your answers; loading the dashboard fetches your stats). It is never read by an advertiser or third-party tracker.

Cookies set by third parties we load

Two external resources are loaded by the app (see Privacy Policy §5 for the full list): Google Fonts, and — only inside the Admin panel — a JavaScript library from cdn.sheetjs.com. Neither is known to set cookies for this kind of simple asset request, but because they are served from Google's and a third party's own infrastructure, we can't fully control or guarantee their behavior. If this matters to you, most browsers let you inspect or block third-party requests in their developer tools or privacy settings.

Clearing this data yourself

You're always in control of what's stored on your device:

  • The app has a "Refresh Data" button on the Home screen that clears the local cache and reloads fresh from the server.
  • Logging out (Profile → Logout) clears your saved profile and progress from this device.
  • Deleting your account (Profile → Delete Account) removes your data from our servers and clears what's stored locally.
  • You can also clear it manually any time through your browser's own "Clear browsing data" / site settings.
