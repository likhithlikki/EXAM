Accepted file format

The GATE question files: one .json per branch and year (for example CS/2020.json). You can pick many files, or a whole folder at once. Questions whose id is already in the sheet are not uploaded; the existing row is left as it is. Questions with a problem are listed and skipped. manifest.json, source-metadata.json and image-source-map.json are ignored.



      
Also accepted: a list of such objects (several exams in one file), or one flat list of questions where every question carries its own exam, branch, year and topic.
